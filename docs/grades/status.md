# Final quality gate status

All four phases reached the authorized ten-round cap. **No phase achieved every grader's required 9/10.** The 120 scored reports are retained, with a machine-readable audit in summary.json. Scores below are product designer / mobile UX / target 18–24 reviewer.

| Phase | Final scores | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Research | 8.0 / 8.0 / 8.3 | Capped; bar unmet |
| Design | 7.8 / 7.8 / 7.5 | Capped; bar unmet |
| Generation | 6.7 / 6.5 / 6.2 | Capped; bar unmet |
| App / final prototype | 7.8 / 7.5 / 7.4 | Capped; bar unmet |

Research still needs direct audience evidence, hosted/dedicated-guard validation, and production search licensing/cost validation. Design retains concerns about composition, reveal choreography and large-text/native interaction proof. Recruiting, paid inference and native execution were not fabricated; Xcode absence has an explicit firstmate waiver for this prototype.

Generation is the main shortfall: some generated options mismatch their card, basis selection can be weak, future assumptions can be tautological, and the final general benchmark primarily uses first options. Paired favourable/adverse/Other-answer evaluation remains incomplete. Exact quoted inputs improve factual provenance, but do not establish causal grounding, calibration or predictive accuracy. The final 20/20 completion result is therefore not a semantic pass.

The app works through the observed flow, but reviewers still want more natural Why copy, improved expanded-panel and large-text composition, lighter memory labels/actions, and cleaner general question options. Actual native VoiceOver, keyboard, notifications, sharing, Apple linking and RevenueCat sandbox remain device/setup checks. Some recommendations conflict with the brief—ranges instead of exact numeric forecasts, shortening the ten-input money example, or expanding the five soft-block categories—and were not silently adopted. See docs/design/review-decisions.md for the decisions and failed experiments.

Final rubric evidence: research-10-*.txt, design-10-*.txt, generation-10-*.txt and app-10-*.txt. The individual reports retain the full blocking notes; this summary does not replace them. This is a reviewable local prototype with explicit quality shortfalls, not a claimed 9/10 release.

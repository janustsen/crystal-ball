The app scores **6.5/10**, short of the 9 bar. The main reasons:

1. **The prediction doesn't follow from the answers.** With the best possible novel-writing answers (30 minutes daily, every day, no distractions), it predicted "You will not finish writing your novel this year." An earlier run with the same answers said "You will finish."
2. **The model has no real choice on the main flows.** For the Spanish, money, moving and growth questions, `server/engine.ts` works out one answer with fixed rules and gives the model only that option to "choose".
3. **"Why this prediction?" reads like a debug dump.** It shows raw field lists such as "Upcoming interruption: none supplied". On open questions the model's own explanation is thrown away and replaced by a template.

Visual quality is strong. Error recovery and the handling of sensitive questions are well done.

## Sharing evidence boundary

Tarot AI's listing describes image export; The Pattern markets shared experiences; Co–Star markets short, self-contained statements. None of the captured previews proves an actual exported card's dimensions or privacy behavior. We could not verify 3–5 real competitor exports without installed apps/accounts. **1080×1920 is our explicit Story/Reels/TikTok composition proposal, not a measured competitor convention.** The app prototype will produce and inspect its own exported artifact at that size, showing the forecast, date, brand and uncertainty label while omitting memory answers and identities. The question is omitted by default; the current prototype does not export private question text. Test sharing with the target cohort before treating it as a proven growth channel.

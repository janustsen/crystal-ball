# Crystal Ball

Your forecast, then check back. An astrology-flavoured Expo / React Native oracle prototype: ask a question, answer one or two relevant cards, reveal an explicitly speculative forecast with a Why and Due date, then return to say what happened.

- Cosmic glass orb, five tabs, local knowledge/history, editable facts, safe-topic redirects.
- Local Qwen through Ollama; deterministic numeric scenarios and constrained generation.
- Optional Apple backup to Supabase with RLS; RevenueCat purchases with server-owned entitlements (mock in the preview); native notification wiring.
- Private 9:16 still story cards and web reveal clips; friend links; Tonight’s Sky and private Circles.

On first opening, a short sparkling intro plays, then a birthday sheet slides up. Enter only your birthday in separate month, day and year boxes; the cursor advances as each field completes. With reduced motion, the sheet appears immediately. Choose Continue to finish, or leave the birthday boxes blank and choose Skip for now to confirm your age without a birthday. You can edit or forget it in You, and add birth time and city for Moon and rising signs. The app is for ages 13+; skipping your birthday requires choosing “I’m 13 or older” before a reading and keeps teen-safe defaults without a Sun sign. Choosing “I’m under 13” blocks readings, including after reload. Existing profiles and friend links use the same age gate. Forgetting your birthday removes it from the profile and saved reading facts, clears birth details and signs, and keeps your 13+ confirmation; readings without a birthday use teen-safe defaults. Changing or forgetting your birthday, or choosing Forget birth time and place, also removes the astrology line from saved readings. Saving an unchanged birthday preserves your birth chart. Forgetting other remembered facts leaves their context in saved readings intact.

After first-run age confirmation, Home offers a short tour highlighting Ask, Sky, History and You. Choose Next to continue and Got it to finish, or Skip tour to dismiss it early. Tapping a tab or the ball also ends the tour. Replay it anytime with Settings → Take the tour.

Tap the orb to move closer and start listening, or choose Type instead to write your question inside the ball. Tapping the typing ball focuses the question; choose the mic to speak again or Ask the Ball to submit. When a provider asks for AI permission, What’s shared opens the full disclosure before you enable processing. For a Person reading, choose Ask about someone and enter a first name; nobody is notified. Answer each follow-up by tapping an option; In my own words lets you type an answer. Reveal a forecast with its Due date, expand Why for an astrological line and reasoning, then choose Seal it for a friend, share it, or revisit it in History. Plus opens the deeper Why, inputs and sources. With Plus and birth time and city saved in You, your Moon and rising join the astrology line for new self, Person, world/sports and Circle-friend readings; these are your signs, even when asking about someone else. See the [social boundary](docs/social/model.md) for the current social features and limits.

In You → The Ball asks, answer optional one-tap questions about yourself or Skip any question. Skipped questions return after the other unanswered questions; the deck ends when all its questions have answers. Teens and profiles without a birthday use teen-safe questions. Every 10 distinct questions answered unlocks a free AI reflection: a portrait, something you might enjoy, something to try, or a gentle change. Allow AI processing in Ask when prompted; if generation fails, choose Try again or Keep answering. Every 25 distinct questions answered earns a bonus reading; a failed claim offers Claim bonus reading. Changing or forgetting an answer does not count that question again toward insights or bonus readings. Open Change or forget your answers to edit them. Existing insights and saved readings retain their earlier context; Forget everything clears insights and restarts question progress while retaining already-granted bonus counts. A few theme-relevant answers personalize new readings about you. When a qualitative forecast names an answer that shaped it, its Shaped by your answer chip opens this page. This makes forecasts more personal, not more accurate. See [billing](docs/launch/billing.md) for bonus accounting.

From a reveal or its share screen, choose Replay for recording for a clean 9:16 replay of the saved forecast. Use your device’s screen recorder; Replay spends no reading and Done returns to the previous screen. The replay and exported still include the headline, check-in date and uncertainty label, with a cosmetic golden label when applicable; they exclude the raw question, answers and Why. Review the headline before sharing because it may include details you supplied. Still cards export as 1080×1920 PNG; the app never posts automatically. The [sample recordings](docs/evidence/positioning/index.html) are experiment preparation, not participant results. For the separate local video batch renderer, see the [reveal-video guide](scripts/content/README.md).

On a web reveal, tap Make clip to record a 1080×1920 (9:16) video of the ball opening, category, forecast headline and check-back date, with an AI-generated label, small watermark and “search Crystal Ball” end card. It uses the app’s own chime and hum; audio failures show an error instead of exporting a silent clip. Show my question in the clip is off by default; turn it on before recording to include your question. Answers and Why stay out. One tap records and opens the share sheet, or downloads MP4 (WebM when MP4 recording is unsupported) if sharing is unavailable or fails; canceling the share sheet does not download. Browsers without recording support and native builds can use Replay for recording with the device screen recorder instead. Automatic native video export and camera-roll saving are not implemented.

Web story images and reveal clips use the app’s own design: the ball in its warm light on the dark ground, with the forecast on a glass card. Native story images place the ball and text directly on the dark ground inside an outline. Gold adds a gold outline, Obsidian a cool ground with a pewter outline, and golden editions a heavier gold outline. The clip’s end card sits on the ground.

History also shows Your year in forecasts for the current year and each past year with saved readings. Plus reveals counts of readings, themes, golden finds and Yes check-ins, with no accuracy score; Free shows a Plus invitation for each year. Recaps use the readings saved on this device.

With Plus and saved birth details, choose Read this month’s chart weather in You for a short symbolic reflection using your Sun, Moon and rising. It spends no reading credit. Each Plus account gets one chart weather per calendar month, kept on the server; changing or forgetting birth details clears this device’s copy, and a changed chart gets its new reading next month. Allow AI processing in Ask first when prompted.

Plus also speaks each fresh reveal’s headline and speculative label using native or browser speech synthesis where available. Choose Mute the Ball on the reveal to stop it, or Let the Ball speak to enable it again; the preference stays on this device. Reopening a saved reading does not narrate it.

In You → See plans, review the free allowance and Plus benefits, choose a subscription or one-time reading pack, or restore purchases. Plans show the price and renewal period; Manage or cancel subscription opens Apple’s subscription management. Weekly is preselected and offers a 3-day trial only when the store confirms eligibility. The preview labels simulated purchases and lets you turn off simulated Plus. See [billing](docs/launch/billing.md) for purchase modes, prices and limits.

For account-metered readings, an interrupted, cancelled or timed-out attempt may still finish and be charged on the server. Retry the same question for the same person, with the same answers, once in the same app session without another reading charge; further retries are charged; restarting the app loses that retry identity. An unresolved attempt can be retried even when the displayed balance is zero: the server decides whether a free retry remains. If it reports no readings left, Plans opens and the app requests a fresh balance. Changing answers requires an available reading. The free web trial has no completed-reading retry allowance: if the server completes a forecast after you cancel or lose the connection, that browser’s free reading is consumed even if you never see it. Pending attempts follow the [web-reading recovery rules](SETUP.md#social-v2-delivery).

The five tabs move between spots in a candlelit library room. Video travel carries you to the selected spot on web and native; web also keeps the room subtly moving when its loop is available. See the [room motion contract](docs/design/motion.md#room) for still-image fallbacks and reduced-motion behavior. Drag the ball at Home to spin it with momentum without submitting a question. Tap it to start a reading. In Ask, typing uses the ball as the question field; see the [typing layout](docs/design/ui-rules.md#navigation-and-the-17-states) for keyboard and small-screen behavior. Reduce motion stops continuous animation but still lets the ball follow your drag, without coasting after release. Native builds draw the same live ball on the room's stand; browsers without WebGL show a still image. Choose Ask this too in Sky to prefill a question. The app also uses [brief feedback effects](docs/design/motion-language.md) for balance changes, thinking, notices and incomplete answers; enable Reduce motion in Settings or your system settings to disable those animations.

In Settings (the gear beside the readings pill), enable Ambient sound for a low hum and a chime on each new reveal. It starts off and the preference is saved on this device. See [orb motion and feedback](docs/design/motion.md#orb-delight) for playback and native haptic behavior and verification limits. Plus adds readings and opens the Inner Library (from Home or You), a hidden chamber behind the room; entering from Home pushes in through its arch on web and native when motion is enabled and the clip is available. There you choose a ball style (Classic, Smoke, Aurora, Obsidian or Gold-flecked) and a share-card style (Classic, Gold or Obsidian). The live ball keeps its glass, drag-to-spin and tap-to-read in every style, and the share card shows the chosen ball. Without Plus the room is a locked door and styles render as Classic. With the server ledger the choice is saved per account.

New forecasts receive a cosmetic golden edition about 1 in 50 times on Free or 1 in 10 on Plus. Its reveal halo and special share card never mean the forecast is more likely true. For account-metered readings, the forecast server draws the edition from its own record of your plan and records each golden draw; a device cannot choose it, and only a recorded draw can be shared to a Circle. Free web-trial readings use the Free edition odds and do not record a Circle golden draw. The edition stays with the saved reading through reopening, sharing and plan changes; choosing a Gold style does not change its edition. History shows every golden forecast on a Golden shelf above your readings, with Share your golden set for a 9:16 image of up to three golden headlines and the shelf count.

Home offers a free, personal astrology-flavored Daily omen, separate from your full reading allowance. Visit Home to generate it for the local day; return visits reuse it. A failed or interrupted generation is retried once, on the next visit or within about a minute while Home stays open in the foreground; after a second failure the omen stays unavailable until tomorrow. Each newly generated omen carries Tonight’s whisper, a one-day forecast beginning “By tonight,”. The next morning Home asks “Did yesterday’s whisper land?”: Yes, Not quite or Not yet, one tap. Only yesterday’s whisper is asked about. Your week in the Ball, a free count-only recap of the last seven days (whispers that landed, readings, golden finds, your most-asked area and a question to try next week), is available every day in History and appears on Home on Sundays. Whisper checks and weekly recaps are in-app only; their notifications are not yet registered with the shared local scheduler described below. Whispers are kept on the device and are not part of Apple backup; Forget everything erases them. The iOS Home Screen widget shows the cached omen and asks you to open the app after midnight; it never generates an omen itself. See [widget setup and limits](SETUP.md#daily-omen-widget-and-check-ins).

Each new reveal shows a fixed Due date chosen by the Ball between one day and one calendar year away; a longer forecast horizon stays unchanged. On native builds with notification permission, the shared local scheduler considers “Did it come true?” on that date and one final reminder three days later, within its next 28 days. Forecasts due together share one 7:30 pm notification, subject to the local notification limits below; use Remind me on the reveal or share screen to request permission. Tapping a grouped notification opens History. For a personal forecast, reopen it from History and choose Yes, Not quite, or Not yet; History keeps the outcome without an accuracy score. After a Yes on a reading about you, Share your receipt exports a 9:16 receipt with the headline, its category, the speculative disclaimer and “Sealed <date> · opened <date> · Yes”. The opened date is the day you checked in; older answers without a saved check-in date omit it. Friend readings, readings about someone else and Circle group forecasts never offer a check-in receipt, even after a Yes. Not quite and Not yet have no receipt, and no receipt carries a score or percentage. Any answer, including Not yet, removes that forecast from pending check-in reminders; a grouped reminder remains if another unanswered forecast needs it. An unanswered forecast stays in History after the final reminder, with no further notifications. Home offers Your check-ins are ready when a personal forecast is due and has no saved outcome; group check-ins use the Circle vote flow below. Web reminders are previews only. Forget everything in Settings clears readings, facts, birthday, birth details, signs and private omen text from Home and the widget, and attempts to clear native check-ins, while preserving 13+ eligibility, the existing age band, plan, credits and daily usage. It does not restart onboarding; readings use teen-safe defaults without a birthday. If omen erasure fails, the data stays visible and a notice asks you to retry; a notification-cleanup alert also asks you to retry if old check-ins could remain.

Home's Today card lists the day's small steps: read today's omen, answer three questions about yourself in The Ball asks (new answers or changes to existing answers count; selecting the same answer again does not), and ask the Ball or first answer an unanswered forecast due today. Earlier, overdue or edited answers still save in History but do not complete the Today step. Tap the unfinished Read today’s omen step to scroll to the omen, including while it is loading or unavailable. Finishing all three steps adds a star to your sky; seven stars complete a named constellation, collected in You under Your sky. A missed day only leaves a gap: nothing resets, counts down or asks you to pay. Right after your first forecast the reveal offers notifications with a morning time (8:30 am, 12:30 pm or 6 pm); only Yes asks iOS for permission. If you choose Not now, Home offers once more after two further distinct days of opening the app, then only the Morning omen and evening nudge switch in Settings. All local notifications, including check-ins and debug test notifications, share a cap of two per local day, including notifications already due for delivery before a time change or restart. The morning omen uses your chosen time; the evening nudge at 7:30 pm is skipped once the day is finished. Check-ins take priority within that shared budget. None arrive before 8am or from 9pm, for teens and adults alike. After three unopened days, evening notifications (including check-ins) stop and morning omens thin out to every other day, then weekly after a week. Opening the app replans reminders and retries failed scheduling. Turning off Morning omen and evening nudge leaves eligible check-in reminders enabled while system notification permission remains granted. Their text never includes your questions, answers or friends. A long press on the entertainment line at the bottom of Settings shows on-device ritual counts and the planned notifications; they never leave the device. Web shows the cards and saves the choice, but notifications need the iOS app.

Open Sky to browse anonymous questions by theme or choose Ask this too. Add my star rewrites your latest reading for your approval before publication. In Circles, save your @handle, display name and avatar before creating or joining a group. Search an @handle to send an invitation request for a Circle you own, or share a private invitation link; recipients choose whether to join. Owners can remove members or transfer ownership, and anyone can leave. Plus members can ask about an opted-in Circle friend by first name. On a forecast, choose Seal it for a friend, approve the headline, and send it to a Circle member or share its browser link; the recipient taps the ball to open it. Browser visitors can open a seal before onboarding. After opening it, or if opening fails, choose Ask the Ball your own question. Anonymous visitors without Plus, purchased credits or a linked Apple backup account get one free web reading, subject to the [web-reading limits](SETUP.md#social-v2-delivery); the age gate and AI consent still apply. That successful trial reading is saved without spending account allowance or credits, then offers installation to ask more. Plus, credited and linked accounts use their regular reading allowance, even if this browser has already used its trial. A network refusal can be retried on another network or after the UTC day changes; refusals are not permanently marked as spent in the browser. Manage sent cards in Circles. See the [social boundary](docs/social/model.md) for privacy, safeguards and limits. If a social refresh fails, already-loaded Stars for the selected filter and Circles remain visible alongside the offline notice and any action error. Choose Try connecting again to retry. The first load shows “Loading your sky…”. If it fails, labeled sample stars and Ask this too remain available when My school is off, including while retrying.

Circles also show content-free check-in moments, golden draws and accumulated glow days. Use the fixed emoji buttons to react. To ask a group question, use Ask for the Circle and review the warning and draft before sharing: everyone in the Circle sees both the question and forecast. On its due date, vote in Circles; private rooms show a sealed status and your own vote until the tally is revealed. Owners can choose Make this a private room · Plus or Open the room · show tallies live. In a private room, the Circle shelf lists forecasts whose due date has arrived, newest due date first. See the [social contract](docs/social/model.md#social-v2) for reveal timing and room entitlement rules. On configured native builds, enable or turn off Circle notifications in Circles; see [delivery setup and limits](SETUP.md#social-v2-delivery).

In Sky, tap Choose my school to search by name or Find nearby schools once, then select your school. Skip if yours is unavailable; Change school also lets you remove it later. Turn on My school in Tonight’s Sky to filter Stars to your chosen school. In Circles, School Circle suggestions offer Start a school circle for eligible friends; share the resulting private link so they can choose to join. See the [school boundary](docs/social/school.md) for catalog coverage, location privacy and invitation eligibility.

Friday Sky adds shared weekly omens to Sky for your chosen school and each Circle; Circle omens also appear in Circles. See the [Friday Sky contract](docs/social/model.md#friday-sky) for opening times and visibility. On native builds, choose Notify me on Fridays in the one-time Sky prompt to enable notifications, or Not now to dismiss it. Change your choice later with The Sky opens · Fridays 8pm in Circle notification settings; use that toggle to retry if permission or registration fails. Friday and Circle notifications are separate choices: enabling Friday notifications never turns on Circle notifications, and Turn off Circle notifications never stops Friday notifications. If omens cannot load, Sky and Circles say the Friday Sky is unavailable. Tapping a Friday notification opens Sky.

In You, Keep your glimpses offers optional Apple backup on configured iOS builds. Use one device at a time: saving replaces the account archive; confirmed Restore replaces this device’s readings and remembered answers, with newest readings first. Restore uses your linked Apple account without signing in again; if Apple is not linked yet, sign in to choose the backup. Switching accounts is not supported. The Ball asks insights and progress stay local, except restored answers are added to this device’s answered-question progress. Sound, reduced-motion and large-text preferences stay local; style choices, credits and Plus follow the [billing contract](docs/launch/billing.md). Completed readings save locally without waiting for automatic backup, including an upload already in progress; failed uploads retry quietly on a later update or restart. If the server rejects a stored session, the app opens a fresh anonymous session; saved readings and remembered answers remain on this device. Startup opens the local archive, then refreshes the balance when server metering is configured; a failed balance refresh keeps the last saved balance. If opening the local archive fails or takes more than ten seconds, Try opening again retries without replacing the unread archive. See [Apple setup and release blockers](docs/launch/app-store.md#apple-backup-setup-and-release-blockers).

When Ask names an AI provider, turn on Allow AI processing before submitting a typed or spoken question. This permits sending your question and relevant answers, your self-question answers for insights, and your signs for chart weather, to that provider and enables your personal omen when you return to Home; avoid sensitive details. The same permission is required to prepare a Star or sealed-card draft and ask about a Circle friend or the Circle as a group. The choice is saved on this device for that provider; turn the switch off to disable submission and hide the omen. Settings → Privacy and entertainment explains data use and forgetting saved information.

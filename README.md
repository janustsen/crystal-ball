# Crystal Ball

An astrology-flavoured Expo / React Native oracle prototype: ask a question, answer one or two relevant cards, and reveal one confident, explicitly speculative forecast with a Why and Due date.

- Cosmic glass orb, five tabs, local knowledge/history, editable facts, safe-topic redirects.
- Local Qwen through Ollama; deterministic numeric scenarios and constrained generation.
- Optional Apple backup to Supabase with RLS; mock RevenueCat pricing; native notification wiring.
- Private 9:16 still story cards; friend links; Tonight’s Sky and private Circles.

[Run and account setup](SETUP.md) · [Research decisions](docs/research/decisions.md) · [Current design](docs/design/ui-rules.md) · [Art and motion evidence](docs/evidence/art-pass-2026-10-01/README.md)

On first opening, enter your birthday and current focus or choose Skip for now. You can edit or forget these in You, and add birth time and city for Moon and rising signs. The app is for ages 13+; skipping your birthday requires choosing “I’m 13 or older” before a reading and keeps teen-safe defaults without a Sun sign. Choosing “I’m under 13” blocks readings, including after reload. Existing profiles and friend links use the same age gate.

Tap the orb to move closer and start listening, or choose Type instead. For a Person reading, choose Ask about someone and enter a first name; nobody is notified. Answer each follow-up by tapping an option; In my own words lets you type an answer. Reveal a forecast with its Due date, expand Why for an astrological line and reasoning, then share its still story card or revisit it in History. Mock Plus opens the deeper Why, inputs and sources. See [accepted scope](docs/evidence/review-decisions.md#reading-flow-acceptance-and-pr-handoff--2026-10-01) for deferred Check-in scheduling and social features.

Home example questions fade inside the fog as decorative whispers; tapping them opens the orb, not a prefilled question. Choose Ask this too in Sky to prefill a question instead. Reduce motion keeps one whisper still.

In You, enable Ambient sound for a low hum and a chime on each new reveal. It starts off and the preference is saved on this device. See [orb motion and feedback](docs/design/motion.md#orb-delight) for playback and native haptic behavior and verification limits. Plus adds readings and Classic, Gold or Obsidian ball and share-card styles, selected separately in You; without Plus, styles render as Classic.

New forecasts receive a cosmetic golden edition about 1 in 50 times on Free or 1 in 10 on Plus. Its reveal halo and special share card never mean the forecast is more likely true. The edition stays with the saved reading through reopening, sharing and plan changes; choosing a Gold style does not change its edition.

Home offers a free, personal astrology-flavored Daily omen, separate from your full reading allowance. Visit Home to generate it for the local day; return visits reuse it, and a failed generation stays unavailable until tomorrow. The iOS Home Screen widget shows the cached omen and asks you to open the app after midnight; it never generates an omen itself. See [widget setup and limits](SETUP.md#daily-omen-widget-and-check-ins).

Each reveal shows a fixed Due date. On native builds, saving a forecast automatically attempts to schedule “Did it come true?” if notification permission is already granted; use Remind me on the reveal or share screen to request permission. Tapping the notification opens the saved forecast. Reopen it from History or a check-in notification and choose Yes, Not quite, or Not yet; History keeps the outcome without an accuracy score. Web reminders are previews only. Forget everything in You clears readings, facts and private omen text from Home and the widget, and attempts to clear native check-ins, while preserving plan, credits and daily usage. If omen erasure fails, the data stays visible and a notice asks you to retry; a notification-cleanup alert also asks you to retry if old check-ins could remain.

Open Sky to browse anonymous questions by theme or choose Ask this too. Add my star rewrites your latest reading for your approval before publication. In Circles, create a group and invite a friend by private link; recipients choose whether to join. Plus members can ask about an opted-in Circle friend by first name. On a friend forecast, choose Seal for a friend, approve the headline, and share its private link; the recipient taps the ball to open it. Manage sent cards in Circles. See the [social boundary](docs/social/model.md) for privacy, safeguards and limits. Without a social connection, labeled sample stars remain available when My school is off.

In Sky, tap Choose my school to search by name or Find nearby schools once, then select your school. Skip if yours is unavailable; Change school also lets you remove it later. Turn on My school in Tonight’s Sky to filter Stars to your chosen school. In Circles, School Circle suggestions offer Start a school circle for eligible friends; share the resulting private link so they can choose to join. See the [school boundary](docs/social/school.md) for catalog coverage, location privacy and invitation eligibility.

In You, Keep your glimpses offers optional Apple backup on configured iOS builds. Use one device at a time: saving replaces the account archive; confirmed Restore replaces this device’s readings and remembered answers, with newest readings first. Restore uses your linked Apple account without signing in again; if Apple is not linked yet, sign in to choose the backup. Switching accounts is not supported. Credits, Plus and preferences stay local. Startup opens only local storage; if it fails or takes more than ten seconds, Try opening again retries without replacing the unread archive. See [Apple setup and release blockers](docs/launch/app-store.md#apple-backup-setup-and-release-blockers).

When Ask names an AI provider, turn on Allow AI processing before submitting a typed or spoken question. This permits sending your question and relevant answers to that provider and enables your personal omen when you return to Home; avoid sensitive details. The same permission is required to prepare a Star or sealed-card draft and ask about a Circle friend. Turn the switch off to disable submission and hide the omen, or reopen the app to clear the permission. You → Privacy and entertainment explains data use and forgetting saved information.

`npm run check` and `npm test` cover types and engine/native-boundary regressions. The project [verification skill](.agents/skills/verify-crystal-ball/SKILL.md) drives the real local app. Tonight's observed surface is Expo web at 390×844; iOS Simulator, VoiceOver and real billing remain explicitly unverified until Xcode/accounts are configured. No public deployment or App Store submission is included.

# Crystal Ball

Your forecast, then check back. An astrology-flavoured Expo / React Native oracle prototype: ask a question, answer one or two relevant cards, reveal an explicitly speculative forecast with a Why and Due date, then return to say what happened.

- Cosmic glass orb, five tabs, local knowledge/history, editable facts, safe-topic redirects.
- Local Qwen through Ollama; deterministic numeric scenarios and constrained generation.
- Optional Apple backup to Supabase with RLS; RevenueCat purchases with server-owned entitlements (mock in the preview); native notification wiring.
- Private 9:16 still story cards; friend links; Tonight’s Sky and private Circles.

[Run and account setup](SETUP.md) · [Research decisions](docs/research/decisions.md) · [Current design](docs/design/ui-rules.md) · [Art and motion evidence](docs/evidence/art-pass-2026-10-01/README.md)

On first opening, enter your birthday and current focus or choose Skip for now. You can edit or forget these in You, and add birth time and city for Moon and rising signs. The app is for ages 13+; skipping your birthday requires choosing “I’m 13 or older” before a reading and keeps teen-safe defaults without a Sun sign. Choosing “I’m under 13” blocks readings, including after reload. Existing profiles and friend links use the same age gate. Forgetting your birthday removes it from the profile and saved reading facts, clears birth details and signs, and keeps your 13+ confirmation; readings without a birthday use teen-safe defaults. Saving an unchanged birthday preserves your birth chart. Forgetting other remembered facts leaves their context in saved readings intact.

Tap the orb to move closer and start listening, or choose Type instead. For a Person reading, choose Ask about someone and enter a first name; nobody is notified. Answer each follow-up by tapping an option; In my own words lets you type an answer. Reveal a forecast with its Due date, expand Why for an astrological line and reasoning, then share its still story card or revisit it in History. Plus opens the deeper Why, inputs and sources. See the [social boundary](docs/social/model.md) for the current social features and limits.

From a reveal or its share screen, choose Replay for recording for a clean 9:16 replay of the saved forecast. Use your device’s screen recorder; Replay spends no reading and Done returns to the previous screen. The replay and exported still include the headline, check-in date and uncertainty label, with a cosmetic golden label when applicable; they exclude the raw question, answers and Why. Review the headline before sharing because it may include details you supplied. The app exports PNG only and never posts automatically. The [sample recordings](docs/evidence/positioning/index.html) are experiment preparation, not participant results. For the separate local video batch renderer, see the [reveal-video guide](scripts/content/README.md).

In You → See plans, review the free allowance and Plus benefits, choose a subscription or one-time reading pack, or restore purchases. Plans show the price and renewal period; Manage or cancel subscription opens Apple’s subscription management. Weekly is preselected and offers a 3-day trial only when the store confirms eligibility. The preview labels simulated purchases and lets you turn off simulated Plus. See [billing](docs/launch/billing.md) for purchase modes, prices and limits.

An interrupted, cancelled or timed-out reading may still finish and be charged on the server. Retry the same question for the same person, with the same answers, once in the same app session without another reading charge; further retries are charged; restarting the app loses that retry identity. An unresolved attempt can be retried even when the displayed balance is zero: the server decides whether a free retry remains. If it reports no readings left, Plans opens and the app requests a fresh balance. Changing answers requires an available reading.

Home shows the candlelit glass ball in a faintly lit library. On web with WebGL available, drag the ball to spin it with momentum without starting a reading; tap it to start one. Reduce motion stops continuous animation but still lets the ball follow your drag, without coasting after release. Native builds and browsers without WebGL show a still image. Choose Ask this too in Sky to prefill a question.

In You, enable Ambient sound for a low hum and a chime on each new reveal. It starts off and the preference is saved on this device. See [orb motion and feedback](docs/design/motion.md#orb-delight) for playback and native haptic behavior and verification limits. Plus adds readings and opens the Inner Library (from Home or You), where Classic, Gold or Obsidian ball and share-card styles are selected separately; without Plus the room is a locked door and styles render as Classic. With the server ledger the choice is saved per account.

New forecasts receive a cosmetic golden edition about 1 in 50 times on Free or 1 in 10 on Plus. Its reveal halo and special share card never mean the forecast is more likely true. The edition stays with the saved reading through reopening, sharing and plan changes; choosing a Gold style does not change its edition.

Home offers a free, personal astrology-flavored Daily omen, separate from your full reading allowance. Visit Home to generate it for the local day; return visits reuse it, and a failed generation stays unavailable until tomorrow. The iOS Home Screen widget shows the cached omen and asks you to open the app after midnight; it never generates an omen itself. See [widget setup and limits](SETUP.md#daily-omen-widget-and-check-ins).

Each new reveal shows a fixed Due date chosen by the Ball between one day and one calendar year away; a longer forecast horizon stays unchanged. On native builds, saving a personal forecast automatically attempts to schedule “Did it come true?” on that date and one final reminder three days later if notification permission is already granted; use Remind me on the reveal or share screen to request permission. Tapping the notification opens the saved forecast. For a personal forecast, reopen it from History or a check-in notification and choose Yes, Not quite, or Not yet; History keeps the outcome without an accuracy score. Any answer, including Not yet, cancels pending check-in notifications. An unanswered forecast stays in History after the final reminder, with no further notifications. Home offers Your check-ins are ready when a personal forecast is due and has no saved outcome; group check-ins use the Circle vote flow below. Web reminders are previews only. Forget everything in You clears readings, facts, birthday, birth details, signs and private omen text from Home and the widget, and attempts to clear native check-ins, while preserving 13+ eligibility, the existing age band, plan, credits and daily usage. It does not restart onboarding; readings use teen-safe defaults without a birthday. If omen erasure fails, the data stays visible and a notice asks you to retry; a notification-cleanup alert also asks you to retry if old check-ins could remain.

Open Sky to browse anonymous questions by theme or choose Ask this too. Add my star rewrites your latest reading for your approval before publication. In Circles, save your @handle, display name and avatar before creating or joining a group. Search an @handle to send an invitation request for a Circle you own, or share a private invitation link; recipients choose whether to join. Owners can remove members or transfer ownership, and anyone can leave. Plus members can ask about an opted-in Circle friend by first name. On a friend forecast, choose Seal for a friend, approve the headline, and send it to a Circle member or share its browser link; the recipient taps the ball to open it. Browser visitors can open a seal before onboarding and then see an installation prompt. Manage sent cards in Circles. See the [social boundary](docs/social/model.md) for privacy, safeguards and limits. If a social refresh fails, already-loaded Stars for the selected filter and Circles remain visible alongside the offline notice and any action error. Choose Try connecting again to retry. Before social content has loaded, labeled sample stars remain available when My school is off.

Circles also show content-free check-in moments, golden draws and accumulated glow days. Use the fixed emoji buttons to react. To ask a group question, use Ask for the Circle and review the warning and draft before sharing: everyone in the Circle sees both the question and forecast. On its due date, vote in Circles to see the shared tally. On configured native builds, enable or turn off Circle notifications in Circles; see [delivery setup and limits](SETUP.md#social-v2-delivery).

In Sky, tap Choose my school to search by name or Find nearby schools once, then select your school. Skip if yours is unavailable; Change school also lets you remove it later. Turn on My school in Tonight’s Sky to filter Stars to your chosen school. In Circles, School Circle suggestions offer Start a school circle for eligible friends; share the resulting private link so they can choose to join. See the [school boundary](docs/social/school.md) for catalog coverage, location privacy and invitation eligibility.

In You, Keep your glimpses offers optional Apple backup on configured iOS builds. Use one device at a time: saving replaces the account archive; confirmed Restore replaces this device’s readings and remembered answers, with newest readings first. Restore uses your linked Apple account without signing in again; if Apple is not linked yet, sign in to choose the backup. Switching accounts is not supported. Sound, reduced-motion and large-text preferences stay local; style choices, credits and Plus follow the [billing contract](docs/launch/billing.md). Completed readings save locally without waiting for automatic backup, including an upload already in progress; failed uploads retry quietly on a later update or restart. Startup opens the local archive, then refreshes the balance when server metering is configured; a failed balance refresh keeps the last saved balance. If opening the local archive fails or takes more than ten seconds, Try opening again retries without replacing the unread archive. See [Apple setup and release blockers](docs/launch/app-store.md#apple-backup-setup-and-release-blockers).

When Ask names an AI provider, turn on Allow AI processing before submitting a typed or spoken question. This permits sending your question and relevant answers to that provider and enables your personal omen when you return to Home; avoid sensitive details. The same permission is required to prepare a Star or sealed-card draft and ask about a Circle friend or the Circle as a group. Turn the switch off to disable submission and hide the omen, or reopen the app to clear the permission. You → Privacy and entertainment explains data use and forgetting saved information.

`npm run check` and `npm test` cover types and engine/native-boundary regressions. The project [verification skill](.agents/skills/verify-crystal-ball/SKILL.md) drives the real local app. Tonight's observed surface is Expo web at 390×844; iOS Simulator, VoiceOver and real billing remain explicitly unverified until Xcode/accounts are configured. No public deployment or App Store submission is included.

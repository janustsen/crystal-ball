# Plan comparison expansion while round48 is frozen

The study compares three structural alternatives within Apothecary at390px, normal and200% text: one ledger, separate drawer slips, and bottle choices. The interactive study is /concepts/apothecary-plans.html. This is a throwaway composition, not a purchase flow or accessibility pass.

The single ledger is the strongest candidate. Every price remains visible in the comparison; only the selected renewal details move beside the commitment action. It reduces repeated frames and repeated terms, retaining plan prices, intervals, cancellation instructions and explicit simulation. The prototype omits the rest of the real paywall controls; a production change must preserve balance, all benefits, packs, restore and test-plan cancellation. It must also preserve keyboard radio navigation and test annual/weekly disclosures on selection.

Drawer slips are compact, but the equal heavy rows read as several primary actions and their simple rectangles conflict with the selected handmade direction. Bottles communicate the room visually but introduce inconsistent price wrapping and much greater decorative height at200%. Neither is a better base.

The ledger prototype uses a raw art panel and a sharp selected outline for quick comparison. A production ledger must use the already processed readable paper, a restrained hand-cut selected surface plus check mark, and measured contrast. The prototype is not permission to reintroduce texture behind typed text or to weaken disclosure/accessibility checks. Production remains frozen at round48 while its two blind reviews finish.

# Crystal Ball UI rules

The captain's Claude Design canvas **Sky & History — UI refresh** (Sky and History artboards) is the visual authority: clean and modern (Calm, Airbnb, Spotify), and every screen plainly says what it does. It replaces the modeled walnut/brass component kit, room lighting, desk reply card and skylight star map. Every tab shows its spot in the library room as its hero (playback and still fallbacks follow the [room motion contract](motion.md#room)), fading to the ground (the 9:19.5 renders fill a 390×844 screen exactly as the reference's top-anchored backdrop; shorter screens crop evenly so the desk ball stays clear); the live glass ball stays where the room shows it (Home and Ask). Fonts and licenses live in assets/fonts/.

`src/ui.tsx` owns the tokens and primitives and `src/Surface.tsx` the surfaces: glass `card` (and `error`), `row` buttons and `input` fields at 14 px radius, `chip` and `pill` at 999 px, and the gold `primary` pill for a screen's one main action; a selected chip fills gold, a segmented control's one gold fill slides under the chosen segment, a selected card takes a gold outline. Every tab shares the header (italic title, none at Home; top-right glass readings-left pill that opens Plans, then a glass gear that opens Settings) and the floating glass tab bar (14 px inset, 68 px, 26 px radius) with the reference's Home, Sky, History and You line icons and the raised gold ball for Ask. Rows that go somewhere use `RowLink` (label, optional detail, gold chevron); cards open with `CardHeader` (display title, label on the right). Text sits on glass or on the ground: the room fades to the ground behind each screen's content and, on the tabs, that ground scrolls with the content so text never slides onto the bare room (the reading and overlays dim the room more strongly); the few labels set directly on the room photo take `styles.overRoom`'s soft shadow. Every size comes from the scale above (`src/ui.tsx` `styles`); the reading flow's question and headline sizes are its own. Copy stays minimal: omit redundant captions and the main reveal’s speculative label; retain actionable consent, privacy, billing and error guidance. The entertainment and privacy disclosure lives in Settings’ Privacy and entertainment.

The app shell fills the available width up to 480 px and stays centered in wider windows on web and native. Content scrolls on smaller screens and at 200% text with vertical scroll indicators hidden; Typing follows the layout rule below. On web and native, shared screens soften into the room at their scroll edges; tab content scrolls beneath the shared header and disappears below the readings-left pill. Home content starts below the ball and scrolls up beneath the shared header, shifting the room and ball target upward together when screen height or a notice reduces the available space (both ease over the room's 450 ms transition, instantly under reduced motion) (`scripts/check-home-boundaries.mjs` checks the rendered viewport and taps). The revealed reading also scrolls over the ball toward the top of the screen, dimming the room as the content rises; this expanded scrolling and scroll-driven dimming apply only to the reveal, not typing, follow-ups, sharing or plans. Nothing may overflow the shell horizontally at 360–440 px, at either text size; rows let their text column shrink and wrap (`scripts/check-layout.mjs` enforces this). Screen titles, the wordmark and tab labels retain their graphic size; task text scales. Touch targets remain at least 44 pt. `scripts/check-layout.mjs` measures every visible label against what is rendered behind it (WCAG AA: 4.5:1, 3:1 for large text). While listening, a notice never lifts the title over the ball. The [motion language](motion-language.md) owns movement. Smoke and embers are decorative and cannot intercept input. Reduced motion stops the shader animation and CSS motion.

## Navigation and the 17 states

Home, Sky, raised Ask orb, History and You are the five destinations; Settings opens from the header gear. Every destination sits over its [room backdrop](motion.md#room) with the shared header and tab bar; its content docks below the spot's focal object when space permits. Settings is the exception: it docks right under the header and groups every switch and its Take the tour, Privacy and Forget everything rows in one card, so the whole screen shows without scrolling from iPhone SE to Pro Max at default text (`scripts/check-settings-fit.mjs`); 200% text may scroll. On short screens, the content moves up to preserve a scroll viewport; notices reserve their own scrollable space, including Dismiss and Undo controls. Main navigation disappears during the reading, plans and sharing; a back/close control remains. Android Back closes the recording replay first, then sharing or plans; within a reading it cancels to typing, then returns Home on the next press. Native keyboard/safe-area behavior still needs a device drive.

1. Home: the room's wide view with the desk ball as the reading's tap target, the reading balance (opens Plans), “What lies ahead?”, the Today’s insight card and a due-check-in action when applicable; see [motion](motion.md#room). Whisper and weekly recap availability follows [user guidance](../../README.md).
2. Approach: the room's Ask close-up of the ball and “Come closer…” before voice.
3. Listening: actual voice integration, transcript, confirmation and Type instead. Denied/unavailable speech exposes typing.
4. Typing ([Claude Design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/Js3cu8UrLGVv1ZAwTrmzcd)): the camera pushes into the room until the Ask ball fills the space between the Ask about someone chip and the controls docked above the keyboard, staying a step smaller than the thinking ball so Ask the Ball always pushes in, and the question is written inside the glass over a veil that dims the fog. Below it sit the disclosure note, the mic (Speak instead) and the gold Ask the Ball action. The Ask viewport reserves space for the keyboard and any visible notice. The layout stays fixed when everything fits; on cramped screens or with large text, the Ask content scrolls within that viewport so every control and the disclosure remain reachable.
5–7. Follow-ups: the Ask ball above a borderless centered question and full-width answer chips with consistent text sizing and padding. When space below the ball is short, reduce the question size and chip spacing, preserving 44-point touch targets; raise the card as needed to keep its choices in view. Wrapped labels can increase chip height. A standard answer fills gold for a beat, then advances (at once under reduced motion). In my own words opens an editable field and an explicit submit action. Progress uses the actual returned total, not a hardcoded three.
8. Thinking: the camera has plunged into the ball, which fills the upper screen; smoke, status, longer-wait message and Cancel sit below it; disclose the [interruption and retry behavior](../../README.md).
9. Reveal: actual engine headline and its Check back date with wording governed by the [voice guide](../voice.md); see [Short, personal readings](#short-personal-readings) for Due date and Why disclosure. Share, reminder, another question, edit and current-session answer review remain available. See [README](../../README.md) for saved-reading outcome controls.
10. Share: compact actual export preview and image/friend/reminder actions.
11. Story: full 9:16 preview and 1080×1920 PNG export.
12. Error: retry, retained answers where available, and edit. No fabricated successful output.
13. Sensitive redirect: real engine classification, no new charge or history entry for the blocked attempt; retain the interruption caveat from [user guidance](../../README.md). Self-harm exposes support.
14. Plans: subscription selection per [prototype pricing](../research/pricing.md#paywall-rules-and-test), packs and Restore. Purchase labels follow the [billing mode](../launch/billing.md).
15. Sky: Tonight’s Sky has clearly labeled synthetic samples and approved anonymous Stars; both sections wait while the first load is pending, then reveal the snapshot together or show offline content with sample questions and “Ask this too” in Sky if loading fails or stalls past 8 seconds; a late snapshot then replaces it. Recovery reveals the loaded section with its entry transition. Refreshes retain the previous snapshot while pending or failed; new data and user actions can change the layout; Circles follows the [social visibility and interaction contract](../social/model.md#social-v2).
16. History: date, question, real saved headline and outcome, plus recaps described in [user guidance](../../README.md).
17. You: plan balance and editable/forgettable facts with Undo. Settings (header gear): notifications, sound, reduced motion, large text, the tour, privacy and Forget everything. See [usage](../../README.md) for sound preferences, Plus styles and what Forget everything clears or preserves.

## Behavioral invariants

The real engine still owns cards and predictions. Known facts are skipped; friend readings never teach the owner's memory. Back preserves prior answers and truncates later dependent answers. Invalid Other input cannot advance. Cancel aborts the client request. Failed generation keeps answers; interruption and retry charging follow [user guidance](../../README.md). The decoder still repairs once then fails closed. Do not describe structural correctness as semantic or predictive accuracy.

See [sharing usage](../../README.md) for story-image and recording contents and the headline privacy warning. The share-screen friend link uses a generic question; Sky prompts use approved anonymous wording or labeled samples. Native check-in behavior and its web preview are described in [README](../../README.md); Apple and purchase setup boundaries remain in [SETUP](../../SETUP.md).

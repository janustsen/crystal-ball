# Visible-review proxy sample

Captured 2026-09-29 from the five US App Store web listings linked in comparables.md. Method: inspect each listing's entire visible Reviews section; deduplicate repeated expanded/collapsed copies by author/date. Include every visible user review, exclude developer responses. No login, installation or paid access. This produced 11 unique reviews: Co–Star 3, Pattern 4, Nebula 4, Tarot AI 0, Oracle Guru 0. These are selected listing reviews, not a random sample or an 18–24 cohort. Ages are unknown and no current complaint prevalence is inferred.

Codes are non-exclusive: P = explicit price, billing or paid-value judgment; R = repeated-content complaint; T = expressed accuracy/credibility belief or doubt; S = explicit sharing/export behavior. General praise alone is not T. The app listings duplicate review text; duplicates were not counted twice.

Counts within these 11 visible reviews: P 6, R 2, T 7, S 0. Fully dated entries span 2019-10-05 to 2025-11-24; three omit the year. One Nebula review is off-product and supports no inference about its accuracy. Zero sharing mentions is absence of evidence, not evidence people do not share.

Decision implications: transparent paid value and reliable editing deserve priority; repetition can frustrate even enthusiastic users. Strong subjective belief makes the speculative label and check-back correction important. This sample does **not** validate our prices, free limit, share loop, forecast accuracy or 18–24 demand. The authorized prototype still carries those as hypotheses.

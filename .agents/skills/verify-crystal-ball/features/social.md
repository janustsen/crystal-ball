# Sky and Circles

## Sub-features
Synthetic theme stars; AI-rewritten and explicitly approved anonymous Stars; resonance, report, block and withdrawal. Private Circles with the [current membership and feed contract](../../../../docs/social/model.md#social-v2). Adult opt-in Friend readings for Plus; anonymous recipient notification. Moderated headline-only seals, claimed by tapping the ball. Optional school (one-time approximate lookup or name search): My school Sky filter and School Circle suggestions limited to direct-invite Circle friends at the same school.

## How to get to it (user POV)
See [README social usage](../../../../README.md) for current navigation and [the social contract](../../../../docs/social/model.md#social-v2) for identity, invitations, group forecasts and seals.

## Driving it with chrome-devtools-axi
`node scripts/drive-social.mjs` uses three isolated browser sessions and real Ollama, completing adult birthday onboarding and handle/display-name entry through the UI. It publishes an approved Star, creates and joins a Circle, resonates, rejects invite replay, enables adult consent, requests a Plus Friend reading, seals it, opens it by orb tap, rejects a second recipient, sends encouragement, verifies the anonymous notice and leaves. Auth/PostgREST remains simulated and is labeled in the evidence. `bash scripts/verify-social-db.sh` covers reports, blocks, quota, expiration, capacity, direct-write rejection and concurrent claims in real PostgreSQL roles. `node scripts/prove-social-model.mjs` checks one identifying sample and one sensitive-input refusal; do not describe that as comprehensive moderation accuracy. `node scripts/drive-school.mjs` (isolated Playwright) covers stubbed browser geolocation, location denial, name search, same-school Sky without samples, invitation-only suggestions, a stranger seeing none, and change/remove after reload.

With the same adapter setup, `SOCIAL_APP_URL=… SOCIAL_DB_URL=… node scripts/drive-circle-invite.mjs` (isolated Playwright) opens a Circle invite in two brand-new browsers: the link opens Sky on the invitation below 12 questions, one invitee declines and the other chooses a handle and joins, and back on Home the ladder still keeps Sky closed.

`SOCIAL_APP_URL=… SOCIAL_DB_URL=… node scripts/drive-friday-sky.mjs` drives the [Friday Sky](../../../../docs/social/model.md#friday-sky): closed state, real Ollama generation through `scripts/open-sky.mts` (clock set to the open week's Saturday), one omen per group on rerun, and own-School and own-Circle omens in Sky and Circles for two users. The native push toggle (`sky-push-toggle`) and delivery are not browser-verifiable.

## Gotchas
Samples are labeled and never counted as people. Unknown/teen ages cannot enable Friend-reading consent. No raw private question or author identity appears in the Sky projection. The AI draft expires after 15 minutes; invitations after seven days; sealed cards remain available until revoked. Free seals are one per UTC calendar week, including revoked cards. The real Supabase transport and native link delivery remain separate verification prerequisites.


## Social v2 drive

`SOCIAL_APP_URL=http://localhost:8099 SOCIAL_DB_URL=http://127.0.0.1:55339 SOCIAL_PG_PORT=55449 node scripts/drive-social-v2.mjs` drives fresh named chrome-devtools-axi sessions against the isolated adapter. It covers real profile entry and handle search, silent decline/accept, a real Ollama group forecast, one Reading debited only to the asker, shared due-date votes, fixed reactions, glow/moments, consented Friend reading, targeted and browser seals, competing claims, ownership transfer and leaving. The driver explicitly advances the group due date in its disposable PostgreSQL cluster and records synthetic activity via the real RPC; those are timing/activity fixtures, not model output. Failed evidence is preserved. Set up the matching ports per the parent skill. Use this drive for v2 social coverage and the current reading-flow/core drives for private readings. For shared-link origin requirements and web preview fallbacks, see [sharing setup](../../../../SETUP.md#social-v2-delivery).

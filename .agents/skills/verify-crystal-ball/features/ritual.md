# Daily ritual and notifications

For the Friday Sky fallback change, Simulator runs are stopped because the environment was unreliable. Use the unit and JS-level checks below; retain the native case for later use, but do not gate this change on it. Real-device proof is deferred until after merge on the captain's phone.

| Feature | Source | Drive | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Today card steps (omen, three Ball asks answers, ask or check in), star per finished day, constellations | `src/ritual.ts`, `src/DailyRitual.tsx`, the You tab once the Daily insight opens | `node scripts/drive-ritual.mjs` (`VERIFY_URL`, `EVIDENCE_DIR`); `node scripts/drive-ritual-boundaries.mjs` for due-today eligibility, answer editing, the seventh star and opening a pending omen on Home from the Today card on You | First driver uses the real local engine and model; boundary driver seeds saved readings, omen and prior stars. Day rollover is unit-tested in `tests/ritual.test.ts`. |
| Notification offer after the first forecast with the Daily insight open, day-3 re-offer, Settings toggle | `offerNotifications`, `NotifyOffer`, `NotifySettings` | `scripts/drive-ritual.mjs` for first offer/settings; `scripts/drive-ritual-boundaries.mjs` for day-3 re-offer and dismissal | Web saves the choice only; the boundary driver seeds prior open days. The iOS permission prompt and delivery need the Simulator. |
| Planner: grouped check-ins, priorities, back-off and pending capacity | `planNotifications`, `registerNotifications` | `tests/ritual.test.ts`; `tests/check-in.test.ts` for persisted budget and scheduling failures; `npm run test:love-notifications` for distant Love dates and native scheduling/cleanup with mocked platform APIs | Due reservations consume the persisted daily budget, including after restart or dismissal. See [notification limits and Love timing exceptions](../../../../README.md). Mocked native coverage does not prove device delivery. |
| Local Friday Sky fallback at 20:00 regardless of remote registration | `skyCandidates`, `useLocalSky` in `src/SocialPush.tsx`, foreground handler and delivery budget in `src/notify.ts` | `tests/ritual.test.ts`, `tests/sky-push-consent.test.ts`, `tests/social-consent-refresh.test.ts`, `tests/love-notifications.test.mjs`; Simulator: `scripts/ios-ui-test/run.sh <udid> <app> "" <out> -only-testing:RitualUITests/RitualUITests/testFridaySkyFallback` checks the Sky toggle in a build without push, Friday 8pm in the plan when back-off allows it, and disabled consent and plan removal after relaunch (no model or social backend needed) | See the [Friday Sky contract](../../../../docs/social/model.md#friday-sky) for fallback and deduplication limits.

# Historical generation review

This local 4B replay predates the [current explanation contract](../voice.md). It completed 20/20, including 11 general cases. Planning p50/p95 is 2.57/4.98 s; prediction 1.88/3.01 s; total 4.44/6.96 s. Inference was sequential before the browser drive. These are completion and latency measures, not predictive accuracy.

The replay used server-quoted context and a model-authored speculative assumption. That explanation format is superseded by the voice guide linked above; the observations below remain historical evidence.

The experimental consistency checker rejected plausible future conditions for lacking proof, as owned-basis-diagnosis.json shows for the election and ex cases. Keeping it reduced completion to 12/20. It was removed after factual provenance became server-owned. Sensitive-topic phrase rules/classification still run on input, generated cards and output; structural bounds, number validation and the one-repair limit remain. Current input/output safety is unchanged. No rejected text is used as a fallback.

The narrower offline card checker only agreed with 7/11 manual labels and rejected valid event timing, so it was not shipped. The remaining option mismatches and weak/tautological future conditions prevent claiming a clean semantic suite or calibrated forecasts.

The later positive-wording card pilot was rejected after its full suite asked users to forecast outcomes themselves. The original proven prompt was restored. At the end of that pilot, Why separated recorded context and assumptions with paragraph breaks and completed punctuation; final headlines are punctuated.

# Reveal hierarchy study (prepared while round52 graders ran)

Room transitions, 200% vertical demand and secondary-action grouping recur in both graders' top fixes in every round from45 to51. The settled reveal shows why: the forecast, the payoff, is small display text with no surface, four equal-weight gold links follow it, and the room orb shrinks to a thumbnail below those links.

compare-normal.png / compare-large.png (390px, normal and200%):
- A current: forecast floats; Why, Replay, Share and More are equal links; small orb below.
- B ball + slip: a62%-width orb above, the forecast on a parchment slip that tucks under its stand, Why/Share as a paired wood row (stacked at200%), Replay · More as one quiet row.
- C slip only: B without the orb.

Recommendation: B. It restores the ball as the centre of attention, matches Home's existing ball-over-slip pattern, gives the opening animation a settled destination, and halves the number of equal-weight actions. C is shorter at200% but loses the hero. Static concept only; production needs the scene orb (not a second image) positioned above the slip in reveal mood, and the opening's end state aligned to it.

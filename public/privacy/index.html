Crystal Ball

Privacy policy

Updated October 23, 2026

Your readings and device

Crystal Ball is speculative entertainment for ages 13 and up. Your questions, answers, reading history, birthday, optional birth details and settings stay on your device. Questions and relevant context go to the prediction engine when you ask. Voice uses your device’s speech service; microphone audio is not uploaded by Crystal Ball’s reading engine. News lookups are off in this release.

AI providers

With your permission, Crystal Ball sends your question, relevant answers and saved reading headlines to Cloudflare, Inc. to generate readings, insights and sharing safety checks using Cloudflare-hosted Llama 3.3. Friend readings also use the friend's sun sign and shared themes. When the free allowance cannot admit a request, DeepInfra, Inc. may receive the same information to complete it using Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B. Both providers state that they do not train on these inference inputs or outputs. Cloudflare normally retains content only for processing and delivery; security, legal and optional feature-storage exceptions apply. DeepInfra states that ordinary Qwen content is processed in memory and deleted afterward. Both retain operational metadata; their inference documentation does not specify a universal metadata-retention period. See Cloudflare data usage and DeepInfra data privacy, checked 2026-10-23. Avoid sensitive details. Revoking AI permission stops new requests; it does not delete saved readings.

Supabase and purchases

Supabase provides anonymous authentication, reading allowances, purchase reconciliation and optional Apple-linked backup. It stores your account ID and usage. When you enable backup, it stores readings and remembered answers, including any money details you supplied. RevenueCat and Apple process purchases; Crystal Ball does not receive payment-card details. We do not add advertising or cross-app tracking.

Stars, Circles and notifications

Stars publish only the anonymous AI rewrite that you approve, never your raw private question. Circle profiles and shared activity are visible to members. Adult Friend readings require consent and share a sun sign and activity themes. Reports and blocks are stored for moderation. Your chosen school is saved if you select one; approximate location is used once for suggestions and is not saved. Apple delivers opted-in push notifications using your device token. Push payloads use fixed generic text and routing information, without private reading text.

Usage and link visitors

The optional “Where did you hear about us?” answer stays on your device. The server keeps only daily totals without sender identity. The AI budget ledger holds amounts and random reservation IDs; account quota rows hold user ID, UTC date, action category and attempts, without prompts or generated text. Link visitors’ free readings use a browser ID, a salted network hash and daily limits to limit abuse.

Delete or change your data

Forget everything clears readings and remembered answers while preserving plan and credits. Offline changes replace your backup on the next successful upload; use one device at a time. Other forgotten facts can remain in past readings. Restore replaces this device’s readings and remembered answers. In You, Delete account and data permanently deletes the account and its server data, revokes Apple access, and clears this device’s readings. Cancel subscriptions separately in Apple Settings. Shared content already copied by someone else cannot be recalled. Contact janustsen@outlook.com for privacy or deletion help.

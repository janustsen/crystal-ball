
## Card-review experiment rejected by runtime evidence
The additional model card reviewer requested in generation round six rejected relevant present-context cards for not proving the future. The same 20-case suite fell to 10/20 completions with either tested local model. It was removed rather than retained to satisfy a review suggestion. Structural constraints, safety checks and the prediction consistency reviewer remain; semantic relevance is an explicit open limitation. See docs/evidence/model-comparison.md for the comparison and preserved raw failures.

## App round six responses
The share headline and date were enlarged, numeric time units kept together, and the headline limited to three fitted lines. Knowledge rows now pair equally sized Edit and Forget controls. Unrelated domain memories are excluded from general planning, with real before/after inference and a failing-then-passing request-boundary regression.

The request to reduce the ten-input money flow to four or five cards was declined: the brief explicitly requests the ten-question numeric example, and silent financial defaults would reduce transparency. Rounding the requested precise money result was also declined. The transparent learning threshold mentions alternative qualifying conditions; it does not claim the user selected all of them. Native 200% text and screen-reader verification remain deferred under the Xcode waiver; browser scrolling and accessible tab names are verified separately. No fixed-layout claim is made for all text fitting in the first viewport at 200%.

## Generation round seven scope decisions
The definitive-language constraint applies to the forecast headline, not to forcing certainty in a research explanation. Removing all uncertainty from Why was declined. Rejected outputs already receive one repair; a fabricated fallback to achieve20/20 would violate the explicit fail-closed contract. Expansion beyond the five requested soft-block categories is a separate product policy choice and was not silently added. Duplicate card text is a concrete in-scope defect to address in the remaining rounds.

## App round seven responses
Export feedback now uses the app's visible, dismissible notice area; the driver asserts the injected failure is inside the viewport. Tab labels remain visible at200% text. Knowledge actions use compact equal-width controls normally and expanded equal-width controls at large text. The learning headline preserves conversational intent, and its Why names the actual starting level and conversation practice; unrelated news assumptions are omitted when no sources exist. The number of generation attempts remains bounded to one repair.

## Round nine grounding change
General Why now separates server-quoted recorded context from a model-authored speculative assumption. The selected basis must be an existing supplied fact key. This replaces unreliable model paraphrasing rather than adding another blocking reviewer. The narrower card-review experiment agreed with only7/11 manual assessments and still rejected valid timing cards, so it was not adopted. This change improves factual provenance; it does not establish forecast accuracy or repair every option mismatch.

## Final prompt experiment rejected
The simpler card prompt looked better in a six-case pilot. Its full20-case replay then asked users to estimate whether they would win a chess match or get a technology job, contrary to the input-card contract. The previous proven prompt was restored before the final general UI drive. Both card-positive-prompt-pilot.json and engine-positive-prompt-full.json remain in evidence. Structural completion alone did not earn adoption. Sentence punctuation and separate Why paragraphs were retained independently of that experiment.

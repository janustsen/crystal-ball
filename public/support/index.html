Get help

Email janustsen@outlook.com for app, purchase, privacy or account-deletion help. Include your app version and what happened. Do not send passwords, Apple authorization codes, payment details or sensitive reading content.

Report a Star

In Sky, choose Report this star or Block this person. Repeated reports hide a Star automatically. For a moderation follow-up, email janustsen@outlook.com, with the Star’s wording and approximate time. The operator reviews reports; report counts alone are not a safety guarantee.

Backup, deletion and subscriptions

In You, use Apple backup to save or restore. Delete account and data permanently removes the account and its server data and revokes Apple access. Forget everything only clears readings and remembered answers. Cancel subscriptions separately under Apple Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If account deletion cannot finish, contact the support email above.

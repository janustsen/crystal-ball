# Hardware expansion while round47 is frozen

Three distinct control mechanisms were compared at390px using the retained room and wood textures: brass sliding latch, wooden peg, pressed seal. See hardware.png and hardware-selected.png; the interactive study is /concepts/apothecary-hardware.html. These are internal throwaway concepts, not production controls or a user decision gate.

Brass latch is the strongest continuation of Apothecary: familiar position change, warm metal and an inset track preserve recognition. Wooden peg is more mechanical and adds corners; pressed seal has insufficient position change and depends too heavily on color. If adopted, give the brass knob a dark bevel boundary with measured non-text contrast in both states, preserve the entire row as the44px+ switch target, and retain keyboard/aria-state behavior. The prototype itself is not an accessibility pass.

No production source changed while round47 reviewers evaluated their frozen packet. This exploration addresses the generic switch hardware independently of whatever scores they return. It does not close the separate findings about centered explanations, enlarged action grouping, foreground contact or connected Circle evidence.
